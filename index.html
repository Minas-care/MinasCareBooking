Mina's Care is a natural hair and scalp health salon in Satellite Town, Lagos. We take a healing-focused, science-backed and low-manipulation approach to growing and retaining your hair.

Our salon products are our own in-house plant-based formulations, so we know exactly what's in them and we stand behind every ingredient. We've worked with clients dealing with thinning edges, breakage and slow growth. The results speak for themselves, check our page to see the journeys. When you book with us, you're not just getting a service. You're getting a team that's invested in your hair.

Short

Medium

Long / Thick
Past armpit OR very dense hair, even if shorter
Extra Long / Full
